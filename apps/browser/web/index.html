Handheld controls

Arrow Up / Right or W / D: encoder clockwise. Arrow Down / Left or S / A: encoder counterclockwise. Enter: encoder press. Escape: back/cancel intent. These are input intents, not cardinal movement.

Controls report their intent below. They cannot enter unported states. A retains its original New Game meaning and B its Load Game meaning on this screen.
